# Research — 2026-08-11: Decomposing v1.1 — MACDh-flip Exit vs CHOPPY Gate

**Hypothesis**: v1.1's improved risk-adjusted performance (2.56 Sharpe vs 1.43 baseline) comes from two simultaneous changes — the MACDh-flip exit and the CHOPPY entry gate. The journal's "MACDh as truth serum" pattern (6+ weeks, live + replay) suggests the MACDh-flip exit is the heavy lifter. Is the CHOPPY gate pulling its weight, or is it a dead letter in mechanical backtests?

## Method

Isolated the two components by monkeypatching `CHOPPY_VOL_THRESHOLD` to 999.0 (effectively disabling the CHOPPY gate) and comparing:

| Variant | Entry Rule | Exit Rule | Trailing Stop |
|---------|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| v1.0 | RSI 45-65 | Fixed stop/target | No |
| v1.1 | RSI 45-65 + CHOPPY gate | MACDh-flip + fixed stop/target | No |
| v1.1-macdh-only | RSI 45-65 (no CHOPPY) | MACDh-flip + fixed stop/target | No |
| v1.0-trail-vol | RSI 45-65 | Fixed stop/target | Yes (vol-scaled) |
| v1.1+trail-vol | RSI 45-65 + CHOPPY gate | MACDh-flip + fixed stop/target | Yes (vol-scaled) |

Ran on 37 live-universe tickers, 200 days, 3,631 ticks. Scratch scripts: `research/_scratch_decompose_v11.py`, `research/_scratch_combo.py`.

## Verdict

**Finding: The MACDh-flip exit is the sole driver of v1.1's risk-adjusted improvement over v1.0. The CHOPPY entry gate has zero measurable effect in daily-bar backtests on our small-cap universe.**

Three things are true at once:

1. **MACDh-flip exit is the real value.** It lifts win rate from 51.9% → 61.3%, cuts max drawdown from -4.41% → -1.78%, and more than doubles Sharpe (1.43 → 2.56). This validates 6+ weeks of journal entries calling MACDh "truth serum." The mechanism is simple: exiting on MACDh cross-under prevents the small number of bad reversals that drive v1.0's -4.41% max DD.

2. **The CHOPPY gate is invisible in daily bars.** On 37 small-cap tickers across 200 days, `vol_20d > 0.035` never fires during an RSI 45-65 entry window. The threshold was calibrated for higher-vol names or intraday bars — it's a mismatch on our universe at daily resolution. This does NOT mean the CHOPPY gate is useless in live trading (which operates on 15-min bars where vol spikes register differently), but it means daily-bar backtests cannot validate or invalidate it.

3. **MACDh-flip + trailing stops don't mix.** v1.1+trail-vol (57.4% WR, 2.31 Sharpe, +6.39%) underperforms both v1.1 (61.3% WR, 2.56 Sharpe, +7.21%) and v1.0-trail-vol (51.7% WR, 2.49 Sharpe, +11.38%). The MACDh-flip exit preempts the trailing stop — it exits on momentum rotation before the trailing stop has a chance to capture continuation moves. The two mechanisms compete rather than complement. Live trading currently runs both (MACDh-flip via decision_heuristics.md, trailing stops via executor.py check_stops()), but the backtest suggests they step on each other: MACDh-flip exits early, trailing stops never get to work. This is a structural tension worth watching for in live trade data.

## Action

**No promotion.** This is a decomposition finding, not a candidate change. The MACDh-flip exit is already in v1.1 (and has been since v1.3). The CHOPPY gate is a daily-bar non-event, not a positive or negative — it simply doesn't register at this resolution. The trailing-stop/MACDh-flip tension is a live-trade monitoring item, not a backtest-actionable change.

**Monitoring flag**: If live trading shows MACDh-flip exits consistently firing before trailing stops reach their profit zone, that's evidence the two mechanisms are stepping on each other in practice, not just in simulation. Track for a few more sessions.
