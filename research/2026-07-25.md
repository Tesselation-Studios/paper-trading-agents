# Research — 2026-07-25

## Hypothesis
**v1.1 (MACDh-flip exit + regime-gated entry, no scale-into-winners) produces higher Sharpe than v1.7 (live, with scale-into-winners) across a broad, unbiased small-cap universe — not just the hand-picked held/watchlisted tickers.**

## Source Observation
Two independent replay runs (Jul 24 off-hours, Jul 25 01:13 ET) on the 8-ticker held+watchlist universe showed v1.1 with Sharpe 2.13 and +2.94% return vs v1.7 with Sharpe 0.71 and +2.25% return. The scale-into-winners logic looked like pure drag. This pattern persisted across both runs. Hypothesis: v1.1's simpler rule set is genuinely better, and the watchlist results would generalize.

## Test
- **Method**: Broad-universe split-window comparison (replay_check.py's `split_ticks_by_midpoint`, variants run via `replay_trader()` directly)
- **Universe**: 216 random NYSE/NASDAQ tickers in $1-$50 price band (300 sampled, 286 with history, 216 in band, 0 excluded as known)
- **Window**: 200-day lookback, split by date midpoint (10,722 / 11,068 ticks per half)
- **Variants tested**: v1.0 (baseline), v1.1 (MACDh+regime), v1.7 (live, scale-in), v1.7-gentle (1.5x cap)
- **Seed**: `2026-07-25-research` (reproducible)
- **Risk metric**: Sharpe computed from daily-resampled tick-aligned equity_curve

## Results

| Variant | Full Sharpe | Return% | 1st Half Sharpe | 2nd Half Sharpe | Win Rate |
|---------|------------|---------|-----------------|-----------------|----------|
| v1.0 (baseline) | **-0.482** | -2.89% | +0.026 | -0.929 | 43.6% |
| v1.1 (MACDh+regime) | **-1.069** | -5.14% | -0.236 | -2.755 | 38.5% |
| v1.7 (live) | **-0.166** | -1.07% | -1.001 | -1.208 | 34.6% |
| v1.7-gentle | **-0.077** | -0.61% | -1.046 | -1.305 | 34.8% |

**No variant had positive Sharpe in both halves. Zero passed the split-window bar.**

## Verdict: **NEGATIVE — hypothesis disproven**

v1.1 did NOT outperform v1.7 on a broad universe. In fact:
- v1.1 was the **worst** performer (Sharpe -1.069, return -5.14%)
- v1.7 (live) actually did **better** than v1.1 on this broad sample (Sharpe -0.166 vs -1.069)
- v1.0 (simple RSI 45-65, no regime gate, no MACDh exit) was the best (Sharpe -0.482)

The regime gate (CHOPPY_VOL_THRESHOLD) appears to be the culprit — it blocks so many entries on a broad universe that the strategy barely trades, and when it does, the few entries aren't enough to offset the losers. On the curated held/watchlist universe, the gate only sees pre-qualified tickers and looks smart. On random tickers, it's a net negative.

## What This Actually Means
The earlier watchlist-only replay results were **universe-dependent**. The watchlist pre-filtering (discovery pipeline, idle_ticks pruning, volume minimums, manual curation) is doing heavy lifting that the strategy rules alone don't replicate. The strategy doesn't generalize to random small-cap stocks — it only works on tickers that have already been screened for quality.

**No strategy change warranted.** The finding is that our replay methodology needs broader universe testing before trusting watchlist-only results, not that any specific rule should change.
