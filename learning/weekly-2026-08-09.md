**Status**: RESOLVED (connectivity restored Friday ~4:30 PM ET). Incident documented, not ongoing.

This is the single most significant operational event in system history. OpenRouter connectivity failed across 6 different model providers (deepseek-v4-pro → v4-flash → stepfun → minimax → openrouter/free → openrouter/auto). ~100 sessions across all cron jobs produced zero substantive responses. Two full trading days (Thursday-Friday) were completely dark.

**What survived**: The book's mechanical design. 15 positions at Aug 5 close, all with Alpaca-side protective stops, all small (1-3sh). No margin calls, no blown-up positions, no drama. The framework's "small and wide" design is the insurance policy — it paid out this week.

**What broke**: The entire decision loop is single-threaded on OpenRouter. Every cron job, every tick, every heartbeat, every reflection is a hosted model call. The sole exception is the discovery daemon (systemd service, not OpenClaw cron) which may have continued populating the watchlist pool — check Monday morning.

**What we don't know yet**:
- Did CLIR's price gap below the -6% hard stop during the blackout? It was at $4.00 (-7.14% from original entry $4.20, stop at $3.95). Two days of unmonitored drift.
- Did the discovery daemon keep running? If so, Monday's pipeline has fresh-but-stale candidates.
- Did any other position breach its stop and get auto-sold by Alpaca?
- What was SPY's price action on Thursday and Friday?

**Escalation status**: Condition was "escalate to Raf if outage recurs Monday Aug 10." Connectivity is back (this session is running). Escalation condition not met. No alert needed.

**Lesson**: The system has no fallback for total model-provider unavailability. Acceptable for paper trading in bootstrap phase (mechanical stops are independent), but a single point of failure that would be catastrophic with real money. Noted in MEMORY.md.
